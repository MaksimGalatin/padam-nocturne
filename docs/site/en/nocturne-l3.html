NOCTURNE Sleep Cycle & Eternal Layer L3

How four replay limiters prevent nightmare dominance, how confirmation-only decay prevents self-reinforcing errors, and how crypto-shredding works on immutable chains.

Four limiters against disaster overfitting

On a synthetic buffer (1,000 ordinary + 612 catastrophic episodes), plain prioritized sampling gives catastrophes 90.7% of batch mass (~181 of 200). With the limiters, it stays bounded at 50 of 200 (0.25):

  • Priority floor P_FLOOR = 0.01 * median(p) keeps routine experience alive.
  • High-priority share cap K_MAX = 0.25 (profiles: default 0.25, ops 0.40, creative 0.10).
  • Post-replay decay gamma_replay = 0.85 prevents one dramatic episode from dominating every night.
  • Importance sampling correction w_i = (1 / (N * P(i)))^beta and stratified sampling across remaining groups.

Seven record kinds & two independent clocks

Decay Δt is measured from last_confirmed_at, never from last_seen_at. Retrieving a wrong fact repeatedly never makes it immortal.

L3 Anchoring: Arweave + Solana + Bitcoin OTS

  1. Per-record encryption: AES-256-GCM(key_i, nonce(12) || content).
  2. Merkle tree: record leaf SHA256(0x00 || bytes), forget leaf SHA256(0x02 || 'AIFA-FORGET|id|time'), internal node SHA256(0x01 || L || R).
  3. Encrypted bundle uploads to Arweave (free under 100 KiB via Turbo); root anchors to Solana Memo (PADAM-NOCTURNE v1 ...) and Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps.
  4. Single-record forgetting (padam forget): local bytes purged via secure_delete + VACUUM, record key destroyed forever, immutable ciphertext turns into noise, receipt exported as JSON/HTML.
